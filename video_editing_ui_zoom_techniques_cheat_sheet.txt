# VIDEO EDITING & UI ZOOM TECHNIQUES CHEAT SHEET

## 1. CAMERA MOVEMENT & FRAMING
- Ken Burns Effect: Keyframed Scale + Position over time for slow, gradual panning/zooming on static graphics or UI.
- Anchor Point Targeting: Move pivot/anchor point directly over target UI element (button/text) before scaling to prevent off-screen drifting.
- Keyframe Easing (Ease In/Out): Apply Bezier/quadratic velocity curves to keyframes to eliminate mechanical starts/stops.
- Dynamic Snap Zoom: Instantaneous scale shift (e.g., +15%) without animated keyframes for emphasis or hiding jump cuts.

## 2. UI & DEMO HIGHLIGHTING
- Cursor-Follow Auto-Zoom: Keyframe Position X/Y tied to mouse movement to track active cursor interaction.
- Spotlight Mask & Dimming: Invert crop mask over target area and lower surrounding layer opacity/brightness.
- Selective Blur: Apply mild Gaussian Blur to non-essential UI panels (sidebars, toolbars) to lock visual hierarchy.
- Layer Elevation: Duplicate target UI element, crop to mask, apply drop shadow, and elevate layer order.

## 3. TRANSITIONS & MOTION
- Whip Zoom / Whip Pan: Rapid keyframed zoom-in on Clip A matched with rapid zoom-out on Clip B + directional motion blur.
- Match Cut Zoom: Seamless cut between two shots sharing an identical central focus point or subject geometry.
- Motion Tracking Focus: Lock target position coordinates to subject transform data while dynamic scaling occurs.
